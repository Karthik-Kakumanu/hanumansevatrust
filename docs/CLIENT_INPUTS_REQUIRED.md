# Client inputs required before production

- Purchase/authorize a high-quality Hanuman GLB with a license permitting commercial web hosting, or supply an approved model and license file.
- Supply the original UPI QR image and confirm that it decodes to `7095999915-3@ybl`.
- Supply original documentary activity photos with category/caption/source metadata.
- Provide a transactional email provider and credentials through deployment secrets, plus the trust notification inbox.
- Confirm whether payment screenshots are required. No screenshot upload is enabled until private storage and access control are configured.
- Approve the Telugu translations currently marked as drafts.
- Confirm official Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, WhatsApp or other social URLs; none are published without verification.
